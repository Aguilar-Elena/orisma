# orisma 0.1.0

* Initial CRAN submission.
* Complete pipeline in three lines: `orm_load()`, `orm_run()`, `orm_report()`.
* Three original bibliometric indicators: WRDI, RCS, MGP.
* Two preventive intelligence indicators: ASS and Bridge Score.
* Built-in normative dictionary with 58 risk categories (ISO 45001 / INSST / NIOSH / EU-OSHA).
* Bilingual outputs (EN/ES): HTML reports, risk sheets, extraction matrices.
* Automatic dimension detection via normative blocks (A-F).
* Manual validation support with Cohen's Kappa (`orm_validate()`).
* Sample dataset `orisma_sample` included for examples and testing.
